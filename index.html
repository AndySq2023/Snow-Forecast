Season so far

Snow depth measured by each resort's ski patrol since 1 September.

Measured depths: ski patrol readings at each Météo-France snow station (Météo-France, Licence Ouverte), once or twice a day in season — early and late season they can be days apart. Forecast, model depths and freezing level: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0). Snowfall is fresh snow in cm, not water equivalent. The snow line on the map is estimated from those depths. 3D terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles, rendered with MapLibre. Live map and webcams: Windy.com.
